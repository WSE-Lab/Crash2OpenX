On August 18, 2023, a Mercedes-Benz test vehicle was traveling on the curved freeway connector ramp from I-105 West to I-405 South in Los Angeles.
Traffic ahead slowed abruptly, and the driver manually brought the Mercedes-Benz to a complete stop in its lane.
The vehicle traveling directly behind it continued straight and did not stop in time, striking the rear of the stopped Mercedes-Benz.
Vehicle 1 was stopped; Vehicle 2 was proceeding straight; the collision type was rear end.
